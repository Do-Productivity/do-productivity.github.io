Privacy Policy.

Do. for iPhone, iPhone Duo and the web · Effective 12 November 2026, updated the same day for founding members

The short version

Your lists stay on your device. Do. has no accounts and never asks for your name or email address. If you turn on sync, your lists are scrambled on your device before they leave it, and the key stays on your devices. We cannot read them. There is no usage counting, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible.

Do. is published by Ahmed Mohsen. For anything in this policy, write to hello.doproductivity@gmail.com.

What stays on your device.

  • Your lists, tasks, notes and scheduled tasks. They are saved on the device you wrote them on.
  • Your calendar. If you allow it, Do. reads the calendar on your iPhone so your meetings appear beside your tasks. It is read on the device, never changed, and never sent to us.
  • Widgets. They show what the app hands them on the same device.
  • The key to your synced lists. It is made on your device and never sent to us. On iPhone it is kept in the Keychain, not in a backup.
  • Sign-in details for calendars you connect. On iPhone they are kept in the Keychain too.
  • The start date of your free days. Kept in the Keychain on your iPhone.

What is sent, and when.

Nothing, unless you turn on sync · the default

Without sync, Do. sends nothing to us. It does not count who opens it, and it does not make an identifier for your device.

A scrambled copy of your lists · only if you turn on sync

Sync has no account. Your device makes a random key and encrypts your lists with it (AES-256) before sending them. Our server keeps only that encrypted copy, under a random number. It never receives the key, so neither we nor anyone who broke into the server could read your lists. We hold no name, no email address and no password, because the app never asks for them.

To add a second device, the first device shows a one-time code. You type it on the new device, approve that device on the first one, and then type the number the first device shows back on the new one. Until both are done, nothing is handed over.

If you lose every device that holds the key, the synced copy can no longer be opened by anyone, including us.

A founding seat or an invitation · only if you take one

A founding seat is a number. To hold it, your device makes a random key and sends only a fingerprint of it (a SHA-256 hash), never the key. The server keeps that fingerprint, your seat number, your invitation code, how many times the code has been used, the time the seat was taken, and the day your app last checked it.

If you use someone's invitation, the server keeps a fingerprint of a random token made on your device, the number of the seat that invited you, and the time. Nothing in either record is a name, an email address or a device identifier, and neither is linked to your lists.

The home page of this website asks the server how many seats are left. That request carries nothing about you.

Calendars you connect yourself · only if you connect one

If you add a calendar subscription link, your device downloads it straight from that address. If you connect a Microsoft account, you sign in with Microsoft and your events travel from Microsoft to your device. In both cases the events do not pass through us.

Sync through your own GitHub account · only if you set this up

An advanced option saves your lists to an unlisted gist in your own GitHub account. Unlisted is not private: anyone who has the gist's address can read it. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to what is stored there.

Purchases · only if you buy a plan

Apple takes the payment. We never see your card or your Apple Account details. The app learns from Apple only whether a plan is active on your device.

What Do. never does.

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics code from other companies.
  • No accounts, and no names or email addresses. A founding seat is a number.
  • No counting of who opens the app, and no device identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or trading of your data.
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

How long it is kept.

  • On your device: until you delete the item or the app.
  • The encrypted copy of synced lists: until you erase it in the app.
  • A founding seat or an invitation: until you give it up in the app. A seat that no device has opened for a long time may be released for someone else.

Your choices.

  • Use Do. without sync. It is optional, and there is no account either way.
  • Give up a founding seat, or forget an invitation, at any time. Open Sync and look under Founding members. The record is deleted from the server straight away.
  • Turn calendar access off at any time in Settings on your iPhone.
  • Erase the synced copy yourself. Open Sync, then Erase the synced copy. It is removed from the server straight away, and sync stops on every device. The lists on each device stay.
  • Stop syncing on one device at any time. Its lists stay on it.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to object to our use of your data and to complain to your data protection authority. Sync runs only because you asked for it. Because the copy is encrypted with a key we never receive, we cannot look up, read or hand over what is in it; erasing it in the app is how you remove it.

Children.

Do. is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app has no accounts, so there is nothing of a child's for us to hold.

Changes.

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. If the change affects what is sent from your device, we will say so in the app's update notes as well.

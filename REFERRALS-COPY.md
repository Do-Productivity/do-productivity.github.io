# Referrals: the words

Written 15 November 2026 for version 1.1. Nothing here is published yet: the
program does not exist until 1.1 is approved, and promising it earlier would
mislead people (and App Review). On release day these go into the app, the
website and the posts as marked.

## The offer in one line

> **Give a month, get a month.** Your friend subscribes with your code and you
> both get a free month. Every 10 friends, you get a free year.

## The rules (shown wherever the offer is)

Short version, under the offer:

> Your friend's free month starts when they redeem it in the App Store. Yours
> arrives 14 days after they subscribe. After a free month or year, the plan
> renews at its usual price unless cancelled.

Full version (support page and the "How it works" link in the app):

- You share your referral code. Your friend enters it in Do. before they
  subscribe, monthly or yearly.
- **Your friend's free month** appears in the app as soon as they subscribe.
  They redeem it in the App Store.
- **Your free month** appears 14 days after your friend's first payment, as
  long as it was not refunded.
- **Every 10 friends** who count this way, you also get **a free year**.
- Free months and years are Apple codes, redeemed in the App Store. Each code
  works once and has to be redeemed within six months.
- After a free month or year, the plan renews at its usual price unless you
  cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
- One referral per friend. A code cannot be used on your own devices.
- No names or email addresses are involved: your code is a code.

## Words to keep and avoid

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "a free month", "a free year" | "free forever", "unlimited free months" |
| "when a friend subscribes" | "when a friend downloads" (downloading earns nothing) |
| "renews at its usual price unless cancelled" | leaving the renewal out |
| "App Store codes" | "we add a month to your account" (there is no account) |

## Open question for you

**Founding members already have Do. free for life.** What should they get for
referrals? Options: nothing for themselves (their friends still get the free
month), or the right to pass their free months on to someone else as a gift.
The words above assume the first.

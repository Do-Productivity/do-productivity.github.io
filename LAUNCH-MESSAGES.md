## 6. Words to keep and words to avoid

These keep the privacy promise honest. A wrong word here is the one thing
that could come back at you.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "No account, no email" | "We collect no data" (a founding seat is a record) |
| "Your lists stay on your device" | "Nothing ever leaves your phone" (sync does, encrypted) |
| "Encrypted before it leaves your device; only your devices hold the key" | "Unhackable", "100% secure" |
| "I can't read your lists" | "We can recover your lists" (nobody can, if every device is lost) |
| "On your laptop or iPad through the browser" | "iPad app" (version 1.0 is iPhone only) |
| "Free for life for the first 100" | "Free forever for everyone" |
